The recorded sounds in bank.bin are cut from two libraries. Both are given
to the public domain under CC0 1.0, so no credit is owed. They are credited
here all the same.

VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO 2 CE)
  Recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell, sample cutting by Elan Hickler.
  The instruments of the orchestra, the drums, the bells and the cymbals.
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE

Interface Sounds 1.0, by Kenney
  The sounds of the buttons and the menus.
  https://www.kenney.nl

scripts/samples.mjs in the game's repository says which recording each sound
is cut from, and how.
